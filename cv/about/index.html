About Ahmad Najmi bin Ariffin

I’m Ahmad Najmi bin Ariffin, a statistician and data scientist in Malaysia. Put simply, I’m a statistician who builds. I care that the numbers are right, and I ship the dashboard, pipeline or system that puts them to use.

National statistics, 2019 to 2024

I worked as a statistician at the Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM), in Big Data Analytics, GIS and the informal sector national database.

Public work I was part of in that period:

  • openDOSM portal
  • DOSM Datathon site
  • DOSM Informal Sector Wing page
  • Presentation on a hybrid geocoding solution, KSS12 CTADR 2023

Industry advisory, 2024 to now

Since July 2024 I’ve been an Executive (Industry Advisory) at PETRONAS, working on OGSE landscape analysis, market intelligence and strategic insights.

Consulting and teaching

I run a data consultancy under MyKetapang Group, building dashboards, data pipelines and web systems for clients. I also present workshops and sharing sessions on data tooling.
